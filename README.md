# Eventide Protocol

A first-person 3D psychological horror game built in Godot 4.x.

You wake with no memory on Station Kairos — the farthest human outpost from Earth. Something went wrong. The crew is gone. The stars are vanishing.

## How to Run

1. Open the project folder in Godot 4.3+
2. Press Play (F5)
3. You spawn in the central hub of Station Kairos

## Controls

- **WASD** — Move
- **Mouse** — Look
- **Shift** — Sprint
- **E** — Interact (read terminals, pick up datapads, flip switches, open doors)
- **ESC** — Pause

## Station Layout

The station is a "+" shape with four wings:

- **North — Bridge**: Command center with Hadid and Vey's terminals
- **South — Engineering**: Scrubber systems, power switch, flickering lights
- **East — Crew Quarters**: Bunks, datapads, personal logs
- **West — Observation Deck**: The window to the void (locked until power is restored)

## Gameplay

- Explore the station and find crew logs to piece together what happened
- Flip the power switch in Engineering to unlock the Observation Deck door
- 10 crew logs tell the full story across four crew members
